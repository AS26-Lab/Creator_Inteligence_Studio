# Script Outline and Production Preparation Foundation

## Purpose
This phase converts an approved content brief into a local, traceable, human-reviewable outline and production-preparation package.
It does not generate final scripts, record media, edit, render, or publish.

## Scope
The phase adds local structure for:
- script outline requests;
- production context snapshots;
- script outlines;
- outline sections, beats, and segments;
- talking point mappings;
- claim mappings and proof requirements;
- scene plans and shot lists;
- shot groups and recording blocks;
- visual, audio, and on-screen text cues;
- platform variants;
- reusable segments;
- production dependencies, milestones, checklists, gates, readiness, risks, reviews, snapshots, and reports.

## Inputs
The outline may start from:
- an approved or reviewed content brief;
- roadmap links from Strategic Planning;
- recommendation links;
- experiment links;
- Content Library links;
- manual review and request flow.

The source content is preserved. The brief is not mutated.

## Production Context Snapshot
Before an outline is created, the system stores an immutable snapshot with references to:
- creator;
- brief and brief version;
- strategic plan and roadmap item when available;
- recommendation, experiment, and internal content when available;
- Creator Memory;
- Creator Language;
- Audience Model;
- platform integrations;
- Thumbnail Lab references;
- claims, fact checks, rights, assets, requirements, metrics, gates, constraints, and missing data.

The snapshot is reproducible and keeps historical context stable.

## Outline Structure
An outline can include:
- outline type;
- sections;
- beats;
- segments;
- estimated word ranges;
- talking point mappings;
- claim mappings;
- proof requirements;
- scene plans;
- shot lists;
- recording blocks;
- visual cues;
- audio cues;
- on-screen text directions;
- B-roll, graphic, and screen-recording requirements;
- participants, locations, props, wardrobe, and equipment requirements;
- continuity rules;
- platform variants;
- reusable segments.

This is a production-preparation structure, not a final script.

## Readiness
Readiness is local and explainable.
It considers:
- brief validity;
- claims and proof state;
- rights and participant permissions;
- location permissions;
- equipment availability;
- continuity conflicts;
- platform variants;
- dependencies;
- checklists;
- approval gates;
- risks.

Critical blockers override any score.

## Versioning and Supersede
Outlines are versioned with history preserved.
A supersede operation reuses an existing valid child when possible and does not erase the original.

## Linkage
The phase preserves source-of-truth boundaries:
- Content Briefs remain the source for brief-level intent;
- Strategic Planning remains the source for roadmap intent;
- Recommendation Engine remains the source for recommendation history;
- Experiments remain the source for hypothesis and measurement design;
- Content Library remains the source for content assets;
- Thumbnail Lab remains the source for packaging direction.

## Privacy
No tokens.
No secrets.
No scraping.
No browser automation.
No LLM.
No ML.
No final script.
No media generation.
No recording.
No editing.
No render.
No publishing.
No hardware control.
Human review is mandatory.

## Reports
The phase exports local reports in JSON, TXT, and CSV with CSV injection protection.
